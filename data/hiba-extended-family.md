# Hiba Fatima - Extended Paternal Family

## Atiya Umar's Family

Atiya Umar is Hiba's paternal grandmother (Dadi).

Atiya Umar is the eldest among her siblings.

Her siblings, from elder to younger as recorded, are:

1. Shazia Umar
2. Sheemu
3. Shuaib
4. Shariq
5. Zakia

## Shazia Umar

- Shazia Umar is Atiya Umar's younger sister.
- Shazia Umar has no children.

## Sheemu and Family

- Sheemu is Atiya Umar's younger brother.
- Sheemu is younger than Shazia Umar.

Sheemu has four children:

1. Khuda - eldest daughter
2. Yaman
3. Maaz
4. Hadiya

## Shuaib and Family

- Shuaib is Atiya Umar's younger brother.

Shuaib has four children:

1. Shannum - eldest daughter
2. Afeera
3. Yaya
4. Abdul Rahman

- Afeera and Yaya are twins.

## Shariq and Family

- Shariq is Atiya Umar's youngest brother.

Shariq has two children:

1. Arisha - elder daughter
2. Azaan - younger son

## Zakia and Family
